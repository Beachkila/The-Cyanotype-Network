Gallery Bench

Square up a photo of a print and neutralize its paper. Nothing leaves your device.
1 · Load
Tap to choose photos, or drop them here.
JPEG, PNG, HEIC — as many prints as you like.
2 · Square
Drag each marker onto a corner of the paper. The loupe shows what sits under your finger.
Estimating from the corners bakes the camera's foreshortening into the result. Naming the sheet also lets the wall hang these at their true relative sizes.
3 · Neutralize
Tap an unexposed margin — bare paper, no image. Everything is measured from that patch.
Nothing here adjusts contrast, saturation, or sharpness. On a cyanotype the blue is the subject, and automatic correction reads it as a cast to remove.
